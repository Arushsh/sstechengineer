WATERPROOFING APPLICATIONS

Solutions for Every Building Area

Application areas and solution types shown in the reference material supplied by SS TECH.

Roofs, Terraces, Podium Slabs, Green Roofs, Balconies & Planters

  • Liquid-applied elastomeric, seamless polyurethane-based waterproofing membranes
  • Single-component polymer-based seamless waterproofing systems
  • Thermoplastic polyolefin (TPO/FPA) synthetic sheet membranes
  • Acrylic membranes for weather protection and thermal insulation

Bridge Decks & Car Parking Structures

  • High-performance APP polymer-modified torch-applied bitumen membranes
  • Composite reinforcement waterproofing systems
  • Integral crystallization-based waterproofing admixtures

Water Retaining Structures, Swimming Pools & STP Tanks

  • Integral crystallization cementitious waterproofing admixtures
  • In-depth crystallization cementitious waterproofing systems
  • Liquid-applied SBR-based flexible and elastic waterproof membranes

Grouting

  • Crystalline cementitious non-shrink grouting compounds
  • Polyurethane foam-based low-viscosity grout
  • Polyurethane resin-based flexible permanent grout

Construction Joint Systems

  • Hydrophilic swellable waterstops
  • PVC-based dual-type waterstops
  • Hydrophilic swellable sealants and polymer-modified mortars
  • Nonwoven polyester reinforcing geotextile strips

Basements, Tunnels & Foundations

  • Crystallization cementitious waterproofing admixtures and systems
  • Synthetic PVC-P and TPO waterproofing sheet membranes
  • APP and SBS polymer-modified bitumen membranes
  • Quick-set hydraulic cement for plugging active leaks
  • Pre-applied HDPE sheet membranes

Toilets, Bathrooms, Service Floors, AHU Rooms & Wet Areas

  • Flexible polymer-modified cementitious two-component membranes
  • Single-component coating systems
  • Liquid-applied flexible SBR waterproof membranes

External Wall, Plastered & Concrete Surfaces

  • Liquid-applied acrylic polymer-based elastomeric waterproofing coatings
  • Weatherproofing and anti-carbonation coating systems
  • Weatherproofing coatings with high-albedo reflective properties
